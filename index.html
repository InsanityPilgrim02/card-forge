Settings

What this is

Card Forge turns an Army Forge list into printable unit cards, and doubles as a tabletop companion: Play mode shows one big card at a time with wound tracking, dice and rule popups. It's an unofficial fan tool and isn't affiliated with One Page Rules. Rules text isn't included, so you paste your own.

Getting started

  • In Army Forge use Share as Link, paste the link into the box at the top and press Import. You need a connection to import.
  • Every list you import is saved on this device. Click the link box to open the saved lists and pick one to bring it back with its play state, with no connection needed. Paste the link and import again to refresh a list; your play state is kept. ✕ removes a list.

Unit cards

  • One card per unit. Combined units are merged into one card and identical units are collapsed.
  • Click to rename: a unit name, the faction label (renames it for the whole army), a special rule or a weapon. Rule and weapon renames only apply to that faction, and values in brackets stay fixed.
  • Reorder: drag the grip bar above a card to move it. The order is saved with the list and is used by Play, print and export.
  • Play qty under a card is how many copies of that unit appear in Play mode and in print (up to 20).
  • Images: hover a card and press Image to pick from your library or use the camera (Capture or Cancel on the live preview). Sliders under the card set opacity, position and zoom. Images are saved per exact unit (name, stats and loadout), so two units with the same name keep their own pictures. A unit with no image of its own picks up the first image saved for that name. Remove saved image clears it.

Reference cards

  • Open Reference cards and paste text into Core Special Rules, Faction Special Rules and Faction Spells. Write one rule per line as Name:description.
  • In Faction Special Rules and Faction Spells, leave a blank line between factions. Each faction gets its own cards and its own title; click a title to rename it. Long lists split across cards automatically.
  • Reference cards can have images too, with the same sliders and Remove button.
  • If you rename a rule on a unit card, change it in your pasted text as well so the Play mode popups still match. The pasted text is saved automatically.

Campaign: injuries, talents, traits & skills

  • Paste your Injuries, Talents, Traits and Skills into their own boxes under Reference cards (Name:description). Each becomes a reference card. Nothing here is imported from Army Forge except a unit's XP.
  • None of this shows on the front of a card. Press the round flip button at the left of the name box to turn it over (press again to turn back). A red ! beside the button means the back has something on it.
  • The back lists Injuries, Talents, Traits and Skills, each under its own heading. Heroes get all four; every other unit only gets Talents. Use + Add to pick from your pasted list, or type your own; × removes one.
  • XP sits at the bottom of the back. Use − and + to change it, or click the number to type one. It starts at Army Forge's value.
  • Each back is its own card. It prints and exports next to its unit, and is skipped if it has nothing on it. Flip and XP also work in Play mode, and hovering or tapping an entry shows its description.

Layout & sizes

Card size, text sizes and colours for every part of the card, an optional Traditional border (default 3 mm, like a trading card, drawn in the Border colour), a Vertical special rules option that stacks the special rules on the front one per line, and No card background / No reference body background options that make those fills transparent so an image shows through (PNG exports keep the transparency).

Play mode

  • ▶ Play shows one card at a time. Swipe, use the side arrows or the ← → keys. ☰ Cards opens the contents list, with the reference cards in a dropdown. Exit or Esc leaves.
  • Wounds: units with Tough get a − / + box per model. When every model is out of wounds, a red DESTROYED stamp appears; healing removes it. Units without Tough aren't tracked.
  • Pin / Unpin puts a PINNED stamp on a unit.
  • Several copies (Play qty above 1) get their own tracking, with Unit 1, Unit 2… buttons to switch.
  • Dice rolls any number of d6 against a target and counts successes, 1s and 6s.
  • On spell cards in Play mode, special rules named in a spell's text (for example Evasive, Bane or AP(1)) are underlined. Hover or tap one to see its description, taken from the Faction Special Rules for that faction first, then Core Special Rules.
  • Spells appears on units with Caster and shows that faction's spell cards. With several spell lists, it picks the one whose title contains the unit's faction name, so name them to match, for example "Prime Brothers Spells". Tap Spells again to return.
  • Hover (or tap) a special rule, or a rule inside a weapon's stats, to read its description from your pasted text.
  • Wounds, pins and renames are saved with the list.

Print & export

The Export button opens a menu:

  • Export to Printer lays the cards out on A4, one copy per Play qty. Fronts and backs are placed as separate cards with the same gap everywhere, so you can cut along straight lines.
  • Enable card backs (Layout & sizes) adds one extra card at the end of the grid where you set a shared back image. When printing, every card gets that image on its reverse unless it has campaign info, in which case its campaign back is used. Backs are printed on their own pages, mirrored so they line up behind the fronts: turn on two-sided / duplex printing, flip on long edge in the print dialog. The page can't see your printer's duplex setting, so with duplex off the backs print as separate sheets.
  • Export to PNGs saves every card as a 300 dpi image in a single zip.
  • Export to JSON saves the list, play state, images, pasted rules text and layout to one file. With the link box empty, Import asks for that file, so you can move a list to another device.

Install & your data

You can add the app to your home screen and open it offline. Saved lists, images, play state and pasted text live only in this browser on this device, so they won't appear on another device and are lost if you clear your browser's site data.

Delete all data

Removes everything Card Forge has saved on this device: saved lists, play state, images, renamed titles and your pasted rules text. This can't be undone. You'll be asked to confirm first.
